Cleaning in Ransom Canyon, specifically.

Ransom Canyon is a lakeside community about twenty minutes east of Lubbock, and it is genuinely architectural. Homes are custom, built into the canyon walls, and designed around the view — which means a lot of glass, multiple levels, exposed beams, and decks.

Those features are exactly what makes the houses beautiful and exactly what makes them demanding. Large windows show every mark and take canyon dust on both sides. Multi-level layouts and open stairwells mean high interior glass and light fixtures that need real reach. Vaulted ceilings and exposed beams collect dust nobody sees from the floor. Lake proximity adds humidity that city houses here do not deal with.

This is detailed work on expensive finishes, and it is the kind of house our service was built for. Many residents here are established and have owned the home a long time — the ask is usually less about scrubbing and more about keeping something they care about at a standard it deserves.

What Ransom Canyon homes usually need.

  • Interior glass and large windows, inside and out where reachable
  • High ceilings, exposed beams, and stairwell light fixtures
  • Recurring maintenance for homes kept to a standard
  • Post-event cleaning after lake-season entertaining

Interior high glass, beams, and stairwell fixtures, yes — that's standard for us here. Exterior upper-level glass on a canyon elevation is a specialty job and we'll tell you honestly at the walkthrough if it's beyond what we should take on.

We're not here year-round. Can you check the house?

Yes. Several Ransom Canyon clients keep us on a light standing schedule while they're away and a full clean before they return, so the house is aired, dusted, and ready rather than shut up for months.
