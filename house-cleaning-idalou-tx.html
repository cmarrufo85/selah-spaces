Cleaning in Idalou, specifically.

Idalou is a farming community first, about fifteen minutes east of Lubbock on US-82, and the agriculture is not scenery — it is the thing that determines how your house behaves.

Working fields mean field dust, and field dust is different from city dust. It is heavier, it carries plant material and soil, and it arrives in volume when the ground is being worked. Through plowing and again through cotton harvest, houses out here take on more in a week than a Lubbock house takes on in a month.

That means a fixed year-round schedule is often the wrong answer in Idalou. Several of our clients here run a lighter cadence most of the year and step it up through harvest, then drop back. We would rather build the schedule around your actual season than sell you the same frequency in February and October.

What Idalou homes usually need.

  • Seasonal schedules that step up through harvest
  • Deep cleans after plowing and harvest season
  • Farmhouse and acreage homes
  • Window tracks, door seals, and entry areas that take the worst of it

Questions from Idalou.

Do you come out to Idalou?

Yes, regularly, with no travel surcharge. It's about fifteen minutes out US-82.

Can I change my schedule during harvest?

Yes, and we'd encourage it. Several Idalou clients run lighter most of the year and step up through harvest. There's no contract, so changing frequency costs you nothing.

Is farm dust actually different?

Yes. It's heavier than city dust and carries soil and plant material, so it settles faster and needs to be lifted rather than pushed around. Microfiber and proper vacuuming matter more here than anywhere else we work.
