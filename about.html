Selah is an old word that means to stop and take a breath. It is what a home is supposed to give you, and it is the only reason this company exists.

Who we are.

Selah Spaces is a locally owned residential cleaning company in Lubbock, Texas, founded by Yesenia Godoy. We are not a franchise and we are not a call center. When you reach out, you reach the people who will actually be in your home.

We built this around a belief that sounds simple and turns out to be demanding: the places we rest should restore us. A home that quietly asks something of you every time you walk through it is not doing its job. Our work is to give that back.

We serve Lubbock, Wolfforth, Shallowater, Slaton, Idalou, and Ransom Canyon — and we live here too. The West Texas dust that gets into your window tracks gets into ours.

How we work.

We look before we quote

We do not give prices over the phone. A number offered sight-unseen is either padded to protect us or too low to honor, and both are a way of being dishonest with you. So we come out, at no cost, and give you a real one.

We tell you when you need less

If your home does not need a deep clean, we will say so, even though the deep clean costs more. A recommendation you cannot trust is worth nothing to either of us.

We answer in your language

Our team are native Spanish speakers. Call, text, or write to us in Spanish from your first message through every visit — nothing gets lost and nobody gets handed off.

We come back if it is not right

Tell us within 24 hours and we return and fix it. No charge, no argument, no asking you to prove it. That guarantee is only meaningful if it is easy to use.
