What a deep clean actually includes.

A regular clean maintains a house that's already clean. A deep clean is the reset — the one you do before company comes, after a remodel, when you move in, or when it's just been a while and you'd rather not think about how long.

  • Baseboards, door frames, and window sills hand-wiped
  • Ceiling fans and light fixtures dusted, blade by blade
  • Blinds cleaned slat by slat — the West Texas dust job
  • Inside the oven, inside the microwave, inside the fridge
  • Cabinet fronts degreased, handles and pulls included
  • Shower and tub grout scrubbed, hard-water spotting treated
  • Switch plates, door handles, and remotes disinfected
  • Behind and under what we can safely move
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped, edges and corners included
  • Trash out, beds made, everything put back where it lives

Questions people actually ask.

How much does a deep clean cost in Lubbock?

It depends on the size and condition of the home — which is exactly why we won't guess over the phone. A number given sight-unseen is either padded to protect us or too low to honor. We come out, walk the house with you for about ten minutes, and give you a real number on the spot. Free, no obligation.

How long does it take?

Most Lubbock homes run four to eight hours depending on square footage and how long it's been. We give you the expected window at the assessment so you can plan your day around it.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most clients give us an entry method and come home to a finished house. You're welcome to be there if you'd rather — some people like to be, and that's completely fine.

Do you bring supplies?

Everything, and it's included in the price. If there's a product you want used in your home — or one you want avoided because of allergies, pets, or a finish — tell us and we'll honor it.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes — completely. Call, message, or fill out the form in Spanish and we'll take it from there. También hablamos español.
