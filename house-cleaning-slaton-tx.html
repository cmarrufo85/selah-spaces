Cleaning in Slaton, specifically.

Slaton sits about twenty minutes southeast of Lubbock and has a genuine history to it. That history is in the houses: original hardwood, plaster walls, hex tile, cast-iron tubs, wood windows, and hardware nobody makes anymore.

Older homes are not harder to clean, but they are easier to damage. The wrong product takes the finish off a hundred-year-old floor. Too much water swells a wood sill or lifts plaster. Abrasives ruin original hardware and scratch cast iron. Most of the damage we get called about is not neglect — it is someone cleaning enthusiastically with the wrong thing.

We work these houses the way they should be worked: appropriate products on original surfaces, minimal water on wood, and no abrasives on anything old. If we see something we do not think we should touch, we say so before we touch it.

Questions from Slaton.

Do you clean older homes differently?

Yes, deliberately. Minimal water on original wood, no abrasives on old hardware or cast iron, and appropriate products on plaster and period tile. We'd rather leave something alone than take a finish off.

Is Slaton too far for you?

No. It's roughly twenty minutes and it's in our standard service area with no travel charge.

Can you help with a house that's been sitting?

Often, yes — that's a heavy deep clean and we'll price it honestly after seeing it. If it needs remediation rather than cleaning, we'll tell you that instead of taking the job.
