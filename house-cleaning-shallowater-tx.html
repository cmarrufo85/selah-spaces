Cleaning in Shallowater, specifically.

Shallowater is about fifteen minutes northwest of Lubbock, and that direction matters more than the distance does. The prevailing West Texas wind comes across open farmland before it reaches town, which means houses here take on field dust at a rate that surprises people who moved out from Lubbock proper.

The housing mix is a real mix — some newer builds, a lot of homes thirty to sixty years old. Older houses have textured ceilings, deeper window sills, wood trim, and window units or older ductwork, and every one of those holds dust that a quick pass will not touch.

For most Shallowater households, the honest answer is a deep clean first and then a bi-weekly rhythm. Weekly is more than most of these homes need. We would rather tell you that than sell you a schedule you do not use.

What Shallowater homes usually need.

  • Deep cleans for older homes with textured ceilings and wood trim
  • Bi-weekly recurring service — the right cadence for most homes here
  • Blinds and window tracks, where field dust actually collects
  • Move-in and move-out cleans
