What we handle after the last guest leaves.

Hosting is the fun part. The forty-five minutes of dread the next morning is not. Post-event service exists so the gathering ends when the guests leave — not at two in the afternoon the following day, elbow-deep in someone else's wine glass.

  • Floors swept, vacuumed, and mopped throughout
  • Fresh spills treated before they set
  • Guest bathrooms scrubbed and restocked
  • Furniture returned to where it belongs
  • Rental items gathered, counted, and staged for pickup

Questions people actually ask.

Can you come the same night?

Often yes, depending on the night and how far ahead you book. Same-night is the most requested slot and the first to fill, so reserve it as soon as you have a date. Next-morning service is easier to schedule and just as thorough.

How far ahead should I book?

Two weeks is comfortable. Holiday season, graduation weekends, and Texas Tech home games book out further — reserve those as early as you can.

Do you do dishes and glassware?

Yes — dishes, glassware, serving platters, and rentals are all part of it. If rental items need counting and staging for pickup, tell us at the assessment and we'll handle that too.

What about spills on carpet or furniture?

Fresh spills are part of the service. Set-in stains and anything needing extraction are outside what we do — and we'll tell you that honestly rather than make it worse.

Can you clean before the event too?

Yes, and it's the most common request — a pre-event clean so the house is guest-ready, and a post-event clean so you never face the aftermath. Book both together.

Do you work with planners and venues?

Yes. We work with Lubbock hosts, short-term rental owners, and event planners on recurring turnarounds. Tell us your schedule and we'll build around it.
