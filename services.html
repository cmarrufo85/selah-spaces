Deep Cleaning

The full reset. This is the one you book before company comes, after a remodel, when you move in, or when it has simply been a while. It reaches everywhere routine cleaning never goes.

  • Baseboards, door frames, and window sills hand-wiped
  • Blinds slat by slat and ceiling fans blade by blade
  • Inside the oven, the microwave, and the fridge
  • Grout scrubbed and hard-water spotting treated
  • Best for: first-time clients and seasonal resets

Recurring Maintenance

The same standard, held steady. Weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly visits from the same team on a schedule built around your life — so the house never gets away from you.

  • Kitchen, bathrooms, floors, and dusting every visit
  • A rotating deep task each time so nothing builds up
  • The same team, so your preferences carry over
  • No contract — change frequency or stop any time
  • Best for: households that want it handled permanently

Move-In & Move-Out

Built around what landlords and property managers actually inspect. Whether you are handing back keys or picking them up, the place gets left the way it should be.

  • Inside every cabinet, drawer, closet, and appliance
  • Appliance interiors and the space behind them
  • Walls spot-cleaned, switch plates and vents detailed
  • Built around the standard inspection walkthrough
  • Best for: renters, buyers, sellers, and landlords

Post-Event Cleaning

You hosted. You should get to enjoy having hosted. Same-night or next-morning restoration after holidays, graduations, showers, receptions, and game days.

  • Glassware, dishes, platters, and rentals handled
  • Kitchen fully reset and all trash taken out
