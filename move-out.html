The walkthrough checklist, covered.

A deposit rarely comes down to whether the floors were swept. It comes down to the oven, the fridge seals, the window tracks, and the inside of the cabinets — the specific things on the inspection sheet. That's what this service is built around.

  • Inside every cabinet, drawer, and closet — including the top shelf
  • Oven, stovetop, drip pans, range hood, and filter
  • Refrigerator inside and out, including door seals
  • Window tracks, sills, and blinds
  • Bathroom grout, hard-water buildup, and exhaust vents
  • Baseboards, door frames, and switch plates
  • Scuff marks spot-treated on walls where they clean up
  • Washer and dryer hookups, laundry area, and utility closet
  • Garage sweep-out where one is included in the lease
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped last, edges and corners included

Questions people actually ask.

How much does move-out cleaning cost?

It depends on the size and the condition, so we don't quote sight-unseen. We come look, walk it with you, and give you an exact number on the spot. The visit is free and takes about ten minutes.

When should I schedule it?

After the last box is out and before your final walkthrough. Empty rooms clean faster and inspect better. In May and August — when Lubbock lease turnover peaks — book at least several days ahead.

Do you clean student rentals near Texas Tech?

Yes — apartments, duplexes, and houses. If you're splitting the cost with roommates, one person can book and we'll handle the rest.

Can you guarantee I get my deposit back?

Honestly, no — that decision belongs to your landlord and it can include things cleaning doesn't touch, like paint or damage. What we guarantee is our work: if something in the agreed scope wasn't done to standard, tell us within 24 hours and we come back and redo it at no charge.
