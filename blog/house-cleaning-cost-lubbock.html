Pricing · August 30, 2026

What house cleaning
actually costs in Lubbock.

Nobody in this market will put a number on a page. Here is ours, with the reasoning, so you can judge any quote you get — including one of ours.

Search for cleaning prices in Lubbock and you will find the same non-answer on every site: "every home is different, contact us for a quote." That is true, and it is also a way of avoiding the question. You deserve enough information to know whether a quote is reasonable before you are standing in your kitchen with someone holding a clipboard.

So here is what actually drives the number in this market.

The published benchmark

Care.com lists 84 individual house cleaners in Lubbock with an average posted rate of roughly $16.69 per hour as of August 2026. That figure is useful as a floor, but it describes independent hourly labor — not an insured, bonded company with employees, payroll taxes, supplies, and a guarantee. Comparing the two directly is the single most common mistake people make when they think a company quote is high.

What actually moves the price

  • Square footage — the obvious one, and the least important of the four.
  • Condition — how long it has been. This matters far more than size. A 3,000 sq ft home cleaned monthly takes less time than a 1,500 sq ft home that has not been deep cleaned in two years.
  • Bathrooms — the most labor-dense rooms in any house. Three bathrooms is not 1.5x the work of two; it is closer to 2x.
  • Frequency — recurring service costs less per visit than one-time, because a maintained home takes less time to bring back.

Pets, hard-water buildup, and whether anyone has ever cleaned inside the oven all move it too, but those four are the structure.

Why we will not quote over the phone

A price given sight-unseen is one of two things. Either it is padded, so the company is protected against a house being worse than described — and you overpay for being honest. Or it is too low to actually honor, and it gets revised upward once someone is standing in your house and you feel obligated to say yes.

Both are bad for you. So we look first. It takes about ten minutes, it costs nothing, and you get an exact number with no obligation to use it.

How to judge any quote you get

Whoever you hire, in Lubbock or anywhere, these are the questions that separate a real company from a cheap number:

  • Are they insured and bonded, and will they show you proof?
  • Did they see the house before quoting, or guess over the phone?
  • Is the price per visit or per hour, and what happens if it runs long?
  • Are supplies included, or billed separately?
  • What happens if you are not satisfied — and is that in writing?
  • Is there a contract, and what does cancelling cost?

A company that answers all six without hedging is usually worth more than the cheapest bid.

The honest summary

Cleaning is priced on time, and time is driven by condition far more than by size. The cheapest quote in Lubbock is almost always an individual working hourly without insurance — which is a legitimate choice, as long as you are making it knowingly and not thinking you are getting the same thing for less.

If you want an exact number for your house, we will come give you one for free. If you use it to price-check someone else, that is genuinely fine.
