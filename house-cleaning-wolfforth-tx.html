Cleaning in Wolfforth, specifically.

Ten minutes southwest of Lubbock and growing faster than anywhere else in the county, Wolfforth is mostly newer homes on newer streets — which sounds like it should mean less cleaning, and does not.

New construction sheds. Drywall and sanding dust settles inside vents and returns and keeps working its way back out for months. Fresh carpet releases fibers. Grout and caulk cure and haze. And with development still active nearby, there is often bare ground upwind of your house pushing dust in through every door seal.

We work a lot of Frenship-area homes, and the pattern is consistent: the houses are new enough that owners assume a light clean will do, then find grit on the top of every door frame. Our deep clean is built to pull that out — vents, returns, ceiling fans, door tops, window tracks — rather than move it around.
